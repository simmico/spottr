About Spottr.

Built in South Africa, for South Africans.

What Spottr is

Spottr is a curated platform for discovering things to do across South African cities. Not a review site. Not a booking engine. A clean, honest resource for people who are tired of scrolling and want to actually do something.

We cover Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban and Pretoria: outdoor activities, food experiences, arts and culture, family-friendly outings and the kind of hidden gems most people drive past without knowing they exist.

Everything on Spottr is free to use, with no ads and no affiliate links.

Why we built it

South Africans have incredible things on their doorstep and no good way to find them. Discovery apps are cluttered, travel blogs are optimised for tourism not locals, and social media sends you in circles.

Spottr is the alternative we wanted to exist: a simple, fast, no-nonsense list of activities worth doing. Browse by city and category, or hit the Escape button and let Spottr pick something for you at random.

Less scrolling, more doing.
