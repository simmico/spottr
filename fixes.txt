The palette has two dark options to work with from that same image — #0B282A is the deep teal-black. Instead of going near-black, let's just lean fully into the teal side and make the dark base a deep teal rather than almost-black. That keeps the warmth of the gold accent without the heavy black dragging it down.

Proposed fix — swap #17110D out entirely and make #0B282A the darkest base, then build the mid tones up from there:

- --color-dark: #0B282A (deep teal, dark but not black)
- --color-mid: #0F3D3E (slightly lighter teal)
- --color-light: #148A88 (bright teal accent, stays)
- --color-coral: #D5891B (molten gold, stays)
- --color-coral-light: #E8A030 (stays)
- --color-sand: #F5EDD8 (stays)
- --color-sand-dark: #EAE0C8 (stays)
- --color-text-dark: #0B282A
- --color-text-mid: #0F3D3E
- --color-text-light: #F5EDD8
- --color-text-muted: #148A88

So the whole site lives in that teal-to-gold world from the image, no near-black at all. The hero is deep teal, mid sections are slightly lighter teal, gold does the accent work, sand does the light content sections. Much more alive.
